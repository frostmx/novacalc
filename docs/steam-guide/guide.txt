TITLE
NovaCalc: production calculator and island planner (web tool)

DESCRIPTION (guide summary)
A free browser tool for Nova Lands: import your save, see every production chain with real rates, plan the Black Hole Pyramid and other goals, lay out your islands like on the game map and write the new layout back into your save.

=== SECTION: What is NovaCalc ===

[b]NovaCalc[/b] is a free production calculator and island planner for Nova Lands. It runs in the browser, nothing to install.

[h1][url=https://frostmx.github.io/novacalc/]► Open NovaCalc[/url][/h1]

[list]
[*][b]Calculator:[/b] building cards linked output to input, with exact rates, shortages and surpluses.
[*][b]Import from save:[/b] your whole factory on the board in one click, with your skills and gems.
[*][b]Goals:[/b] pick the Black Hole Pyramid, Nova Gateway or your own list and get the chain built for you.
[*][b]Architect:[/b] your islands as on the game map, buildings at their real size and place, transporters between islands.
[*][b]Save editor:[/b] change that layout and download your world with the changes.
[/list]

The numbers come from the game files and were checked against the game code and timed in game, not taken from the wiki. Your save stays on your computer: the page reads it locally and sends it nowhere.

[previewimg=48055339;sizeThumb,floatLeft;01-import-by-island.png][/previewimg]


=== SECTION: Quick start: import your factory ===

Click [b]Import from save[/b] and pick your world file:
[code]%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\BEHEMUTT\Nova Lands\Worlds\1.1.0\WORLD000.dat[/code]
The game writes it when you sleep and on "Save and exit".

Every production building lands on the board, grouped into one card per island and setup ("Advanced Furnace ×4, Iron Ingot, Super Lubricant") and linked into chains. Learned skills and the gems in your Gem Overclocker are applied. The Workbench and the Animal Cloning Machine are skipped: they are manual tools.

The board is kept in the browser. [b]Export JSON[/b] saves it to a file, [b]Import JSON[/b] loads it back.


=== SECTION: Cards and links ===

A card is a group of identical buildings running one recipe: the count, the recipe, the real cycle time with all bonuses (hover the speed to see what adds up), lubricant, fertilizer or watering where the building has them, inputs on the left and outputs on the right.

[previewimg=48055341;sizeFull,floatLeft;02-cards-and-producer-menu.png][/previewimg]

Drag from one port to another to link two cards. Drag a port into empty space or click the [b]+[/b] next to it to get the buildings that make or use that item: the one you pick comes already linked and sized to cover the missing amount.

Green means balanced, red short (the number is how much is missing), orange a surplus nothing uses, grey not connected. "Inputs cover 66%" on a card means it only runs at 66% of its speed.


=== SECTION: Summary ===

The right panel totals the board: buildings, energy and every item made and used, worst shortages first. Hover an item to light up the cards that make it (green) and use it (orange); click it to zoom to them.

[previewimg=48055342;sizeFull,floatLeft;03-summary-highlight.png][/previewimg]


=== SECTION: Bonuses: skills and gems ===

[b]Bonuses[/b] lists the skills and upgrades that change production and a gem slot for each Gem Overclocker group. Import fills them in; tick them by hand to see what a skill or a better gem would give.

[previewimg=48055343;sizeFull,floatLeft;04-bonuses.png][/previewimg]

[list]
[*]Speed = base speed × (1 + skills + gem + lubricant). Furnaces, refineries and assemblers have a base speed of 0.8.
[*]Lubricant counts as ×3, ×4 or ×16 and burns in real time while the building works.
[*]Fertilizer speeds up growth; plants need water to grow, renewable ones regrow without it.
[*]Fuel skills make fuel last longer, Solar Power doubles solar panels.
[*]Energy is shared per island, as in the game.
[/list]


=== SECTION: Goals and "Build chain" ===

Open [b]Goals[/b] at the top of the left list, pick a Black Hole Pyramid or Nova Gateway stage, Kruwak's Spaceship or a [b]Custom goal[/b], and say how often you want it ("every 10 h").

[b]Build chain[/b] on the goal card adds every building needed down to raw resources, reusing what is already there. It picks the fastest recipe that doesn't eat packages: Mega recipes take 100-item packages and are rarely worth it. Drills, farms and creatures are left to you; their products show up as raw resources.

[previewimg=48055344;sizeFull,floatLeft;05-goal-build-chain.png][/previewimg]


=== SECTION: Islands on the board ===

[b]By island[/b] puts each island in its own frame with its energy balance, [b]Whole chain[/b] shows one chain with the island written on every card. Each view keeps its own layout; [b]Arrange[/b] lays out the current one again.

[previewimg=48055345;sizeFull,floatLeft;06-whole-chain.png][/previewimg]

To move a card to another island, drop it into that island's frame. [b]+ Island[/b] adds an empty one; double-click a title to rename it. Links between islands are dashed.


=== SECTION: Architect: your islands as in the game ===

[b]Architect[/b] (top left) shows your islands as on the game map, with every building where it stands in the game and at its real size. Production buildings show what they make.

[previewimg=48055346;sizeFull,floatLeft;07-architect-map.png][/previewimg]

Pick a building in the left list and click on an island to place it: it snaps to the game's grid, red means it doesn't fit. Shift-click places more, Esc stops. Drag to move, Delete to remove. An empty hexagon with a "+" becomes a new island.

The map and the calculator are the same plan: placing a building adds one to its card, deleting takes one away. Buildings a card has but the map doesn't wait under [b]Not placed[/b] at the top of the list.

[previewimg=48055350;sizeFull,floatLeft;10-architect-placing.png][/previewimg]

Click a building to see lines to its suppliers (green) and consumers (orange) and its card right next to it, where you can change the recipe, lubricant or count. Clicking a port of that card lets you pick a new producer and place it straight away.

[previewimg=48055347;sizeFull,floatLeft;08-architect-selected-building.png][/previewimg]


=== SECTION: Transporters ===

Pick [b]Transporter Arm[/b] under Infrastructure and click near a shore: the arm takes the nearest slot and its partner appears on the island across. Drag an arm along the shore to move the pair. Select an arm to choose what it carries.

[previewimg=48055349;sizeFull,floatLeft;09-transporters.png][/previewimg]

Links between islands only carry what the transporters can move: 60 items per minute each (measured in game), also through other islands. A link no transporter serves is a red dashed line.


=== SECTION: Save editor ===

[h1]⚠ USE AT YOUR OWN RISK — ALWAYS BACK UP YOUR SAVE! ⚠[/h1]

After [b]Import from save[/b] the changes you make in the architect can go back into your world: moved, new and deleted buildings, recipes, transporters. An Advanced Farm moves with its plots and plants. Items left from an old recipe drop on the ground when the world loads.

Rocks, bushes and trees are on the map too. You can't build on top of them, but you can drag them aside.

[previewimg=48058158;sizeFull,floatLeft;11-save-editor-rocks.png][/previewimg]

New buildings are free, but only types your save already has can be placed; the rest are greyed out. NPCs, shops, landmarks and the special islands stay as they are.

To get the changes into the game:
[olist]
[*]Click [b]Download save[/b] and check the list of changes.
[*]Go to the main menu in the game.
[*]Back up your old WORLD000.dat and put the downloaded file in its place.
[*]Load the world.
[/olist]


=== SECTION: Good to know ===

[list]
[*]A drill mines one item per 0.11 s (545/min, about 514 at 60 FPS) and uses a Titanium Drill Bit every 225 s. What it can mine depends on the island.
[*]Gem slots 1–4 of the Gem Overclocker each boost their own group of buildings, as listed in Bonuses.
[*]The board lives in your browser only: use [b]Export JSON[/b] to keep a copy.
[/list]
