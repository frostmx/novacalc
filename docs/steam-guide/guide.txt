TITLE
NovaCalc: production calculator and island planner (web tool)

DESCRIPTION (guide summary)
A free browser tool for planning Nova Lands factories: import your save, see every chain with real rates, fix
shortages, plan goals like the Black Hole Pyramid, and lay out islands, buildings and transporters exactly as in
the game.

=== SECTION: What is NovaCalc ===

[b]NovaCalc[/b] is a free production chain calculator and island planner for Nova Lands. It runs in your web
browser — nothing to install:

[h1][url=https://frostmx.github.io/novacalc/]► Open NovaCalc[/url][/h1]

[list]
[*][b]Calculator[/b] — put building cards on a board, connect outputs to inputs and see exact rates, shortages
and surpluses.
[*][b]Import from save[/b] — load your world and get your whole factory, with your skills and gems, in one click.
[*][b]Goals[/b] — choose a delivery target (Black Hole Pyramid, Nova Gateway, …) and let it build the chain.
[*][b]Architect[/b] — your islands as on the game map, buildings at their real size and position, transporters
between islands.
[/list]

All numbers come from the game files and were checked against the game code and timed in-game tests — not
copied from the wiki.

Your save never leaves your computer: the page reads it in the browser and sends it nowhere.

[previewimg=48055339;sizeThumb,floatLeft;01-import-by-island.png][/previewimg]


=== SECTION: Quick start: import your factory ===

[olist]
[*]Open the site and click [b]Import from save[/b] (blue button at the top).
[*]Pick your world file. On Windows it is here (paste the path into the file dialog):
[code]%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\BEHEMUTT\Nova Lands\Worlds\1.1.0\WORLD000.dat[/code]
The game writes it when you sleep and on "Save and exit", so save first if you want the latest state.
[*]Done. Every production building appears on the board, merged into one card per island and setup
("Advanced Furnace ×4, Iron Ingot, Super Lubricant"), linked into chains. Your learned skills and the gems in your
Gem Overclocker are applied too.
[/olist]

A short message tells you how many buildings were placed and which ones were left out (the Workbench and the
Animal Cloning Machine are manual tools and are skipped).

The board is kept in your browser between visits. [b]Export JSON[/b] / [b]Import JSON[/b] save and load it as a
file, [b]Clear[/b] starts over.


=== SECTION: Cards, ports and links ===

Every card is a group of identical buildings running one recipe.

[previewimg=48055341;sizeFull,floatLeft;02-cards-and-producer-menu.png][/previewimg]

[list]
[*][b]Header[/b] — building, how many of them (− / + or type a number), × removes the card.
[*][b]Recipe[/b] — what the buildings make. Farms choose a plant, the Animal Barn a creature, generators show the
energy they produce.
[*][b]Cycle and speed[/b] — real cycle time with all bonuses. Hover the speed to see where it comes from
(building speed, skills, gem, lubricant).
[*][b]Options[/b] — lubricant, fertilizer, watering, number of creatures, depending on the building.
[*][b]Ports[/b] — inputs on the left, outputs on the right, with the rate for the whole card.
[/list]

[h3]Connecting[/h3]
[list]
[*]Drag from a port to a port of another card to link them.
[*]Drag from a port onto empty space, or click the small [b]+[/b] next to it, to get a list of buildings that
make (or use) that item. Pick one and it is added already linked and sized to cover the missing amount.
[*]Click a link and press Delete to remove it.
[/list]

[h3]Colours[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Green[/b] port or link — balanced.
[*][b]Red[/b] — short: the number shows how much is missing. "Inputs cover 66%" on a card means it can only
run at that share of its speed.
[*][b]Orange[/b] — surplus that nothing uses.
[*][b]Grey[/b] — not connected (treated as bought in / brought by hand).
[/list]

[b]Rate[/b] at the top switches everything between per second, per minute and per hour.


=== SECTION: Summary ===

The panel on the right totals the whole board: buildings, energy used / produced, and every item made and used,
worst shortages first.

[list]
[*][b]Hover an item[/b] — cards that make it are outlined green, cards that use it orange, everything else
fades.
[*][b]Click an item[/b] — the view zooms to exactly those cards.
[/list]

[previewimg=48055342;sizeFull,floatLeft;03-summary-highlight.png][/previewimg]


=== SECTION: Bonuses: skills and gems ===

[b]Bonuses[/b] (top bar) lists the skills and upgrades that change production, and one gem slot per Gem
Overclocker group. Import from save fills them in; you can also tick and change them by hand to see what a skill
or a better gem would give.

[previewimg=48055343;sizeFull,floatLeft;04-bonuses.png][/previewimg]

What is taken into account:
[list]
[*]Building speed: [i]base speed × (1 + skills + gem + lubricant)[/i]. Furnaces, refineries and assemblers have a
base speed of 0.8, not 1.
[*]Lubricants multiply as the game says (×3, ×4, ×16) and are used up in real time while the building works.
[*]Agriculture-type skills add extra output per cycle.
[*]Farms: fertilizer speeds growth, water is needed for growing, renewable plants regrow without water.
[*]Generators: fuel lasts longer with the fuel skills, solar panels double with Solar Power.
[*]Energy is shared per island, exactly like in the game.
[/list]


=== SECTION: Goals and "Build chain" ===

Open [b]Goals[/b] at the top of the left list. Pick a delivery point (Black Hole Pyramid stages, Nova Gateway
stages, Kruwak's Spaceship) or a [b]Custom goal[/b] with your own items, and say how often you want it
(for example "every 10 h").

Click [b]Build chain[/b] on the goal card: the calculator adds every building needed, all the way down to raw
resources, reusing the buildings already in the chain. It prefers the fastest recipe that does not eat packages
(Mega recipes consume 100-item packages and are rarely worth it).

Drills, farms, creatures and manual crafting are left to you: their products are listed as raw resources in the
message.

[previewimg=48055344;sizeFull,floatLeft;05-goal-build-chain.png][/previewimg]


=== SECTION: Islands on the board ===

Imported cards keep their island. The top bar has two views:
[list]
[*][b]By island[/b] — each island in its own frame with its energy balance.
[*][b]Whole chain[/b] — one production chain, islands not separated (each card still shows its island).
[*][b]Arrange[/b] — lays out the current view automatically.
[/list]

Each view remembers its own card positions, so untangling one does not mess up the other.

[previewimg=48055345;sizeFull,floatLeft;06-whole-chain.png][/previewimg]

In the island view:
[list]
[*][b]+ Island[/b] adds an empty island frame.
[*]Drop a card into another island's frame to move it there. Dragging it anywhere else keeps it on its island
(the frame just grows). Hold [b]Alt[/b] while dropping outside every frame to take it off the islands.
[*]Double-click an island title to rename it, × deletes the island (its cards stay).
[*]Links between different islands are dashed.
[/list]


=== SECTION: Architect: your islands as in the game ===

Switch to [b]Architect[/b] at the top left. Your islands are shown as on the game's map, in their biome
colours, and every building of your save stands where it stands in the game, at its real size. Production
buildings show the item they make; storage, NPCs and other buildings are grey.

[previewimg=48055346;sizeFull,floatLeft;07-architect-map.png][/previewimg]

[list]
[*][b]Add an island[/b]: click an empty hexagon with a "+" and choose the island type.
[*][b]Place a building[/b]: pick it in the left list, then click on an island. A preview shows where it goes
(red = blocked); it snaps to the game's grid. Shift-click to keep placing, Esc or right click to stop.
[*][b]Move[/b]: drag a building. [b]Delete[/b]: select it and press Delete.
[*][b]Rename / delete an island[/b]: double-click its ground / click × next to its name.
[/list]

The architect and the calculator are one plan: every building on an island belongs to its card on the board.
Placing a building adds one to the card, deleting removes one, moving it to another island moves it to that
island's card. If a card has more buildings than are placed, they wait in [b]Not placed[/b] at the top of the
left list — click there and place them.

[previewimg=48055350;sizeFull,floatLeft;10-architect-placing.png][/previewimg]


=== SECTION: Editing from the map ===

Click a building to select it:
[list]
[*]Lines go to the buildings that supply it ([b]green[/b]) and that take its output ([b]orange[/b]), with the
item on each line.
[*]Its card from the calculator opens next to it: change the recipe, lubricant or count right there.
[*]Click a port (the dot on the left or right of the card) to choose a producer or consumer for that item — the
new buildings are placed straight away with your next clicks.
[*]Double-click a building to jump to its card in the calculator.
[/list]

[previewimg=48055347;sizeFull,floatLeft;08-architect-selected-building.png][/previewimg]


=== SECTION: Transporters ===

A transporter is a pair of arms on the shores of two neighbouring islands. In the architect:
[list]
[*]Pick [b]Transporter Arm[/b] under Infrastructure and click near an island's shore — the arm snaps to the
nearest shore slot and its partner appears on the island across.
[*]Drag an arm along the shore to move the pair; across another edge the partner moves to that neighbour.
[*]Each arm shows the item it carries; select an arm to change the item.
[/list]

[previewimg=48055349;sizeFull,floatLeft;09-transporters.png][/previewimg]

Transporters count in the calculation: a link between cards on different islands only carries what the
transporters for that item can move — [b]60 items per minute per transporter[/b] (measured in game), also
through other islands. A link that no transporter serves carries nothing and is drawn as a red dashed line;
in the calculator, hover it to see which transporter is missing.


=== SECTION: Good to know ===

[list]
[*][b]Drills[/b] mine 1 item per 0.11 s (545/min) and use one Titanium Drill Bit per 225 s of work. What a drill
can mine depends on the island; a drill on the wrong island is marked red. The game gives at most one item per
frame, so at 60 FPS it is closer to 514/min (shown in the tooltip).
[*][b]Mega recipes[/b] eat packages (1 package = 100 items): fast per building, very expensive in materials.
[*][b]Gem Overclocker[/b]: gem slot 1, 2, 3, 4 boosts its own building group, as listed in Bonuses.
[*]The board is stored in your browser only. Use [b]Export JSON[/b] to back it up or move it to another PC.
[/list]
